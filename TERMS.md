# Terms of use — SUGAR Wallet

These are the terms a user agrees to in the app, in full. They are short because
the app is small, and because a long document that nobody reads protects nobody.

## What the app does

1. It creates a **Sugarchain wallet on the device**. The private key is generated
   locally with the platform's secure random source and stored in the platform
   keystore. It is never transmitted anywhere.
2. It mines SUGAR cryptocurrency using the device's processing power, **into that
   wallet**. The coins are the user's.
3. Mining runs only while the user has agreed to it, and stops immediately when the
   user stops it. It pauses automatically on heat, low battery, mobile data and the
   daily time cap.

## What the app does not do

- It does not mine to any address other than the user's own.
- It does not send the private key, the wallet, or any personal information to the
  developer or to anyone else.
- It does not collect analytics, crash reports or usage data.
- It does not hide, silence or delay the mining notification, and it does not
  restart mining after the user has stopped it without the user starting it again.

## The user's responsibilities

1. **Keep the backup.** Whoever holds the private key holds the coins. If the
   backup is lost, the SUGAR at that address can never be moved again.
2. **Expect small amounts.** A phone produces on the order of 100–400 hashes per
   second. Revenues are typically cents per day or less, and the value of SUGAR is
   not guaranteed, supported or insured by anyone.
3. **Understand the trade.** Mining consumes electricity and battery cycles. The
   app limits this, but it is not free.
4. **Distribute legally.** Google Play prohibits on-device mining; use sideloading,
   enterprise distribution or another store that permits it.

## No warranty

The app is provided "as is". Mining may produce nothing at all. Pools may be
unavailable, the network hashrate may make a phone's contribution negligible, and
the coin may be worthless. Nothing here is financial advice, and no earnings are
promised or implied.

## Changes

If the disclosure the user agreed to changes, the app asks again before mining
continues — the version of the notice, not the date, is what the agreement is
recorded against.
