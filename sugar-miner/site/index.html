What to expect. At this pool a share is worth shareDiff = 2^240/value (that is the pool's own formula) and it accepts when shareDiff ≥ 0.99 × difficulty. At the starting difficulty 0.5 a share needs ≈3.2×10⁴ yespower hashes, so a wasm tab does one every few minutes — and the pool lowers your difficulty as soon as you land a couple of them (measured live: 0.5 → 0.121 after two accepted shares, which makes a share ≈7.8×10³ hashes, i.e. roughly one every 30 s here). Shares for a job older than ~10 s come back as stale share, so hits are submitted the instant they are found. For many more shares per hour run a native miner (sugarmaker, cpuminer-opt) against the same address with -a yespowersugar -o stratum+tcp://stratum.poolab.org:8451; the pool accepts those too (or run tools/run-sugarmaker.sh on any Linux/Raspberry Pi box).
